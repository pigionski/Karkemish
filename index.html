Sail the Syrian
Euphrates

A private boat journey through 5,000 years of history, two monumental archaeological sites, wild river birds and breathtaking landscapes along the Middle Euphrates.

The Sites

Your large boat, comfortable for up to 12 people and fully equipped with every safety features, takes you to two extraordinary places most travellers never reach.

5,200 years old · Late Chalcolithic

Jebel Aruda

Founded by colonists from Uruk in southern Iraq, Jebel Aruda is one of the oldest urban settlements along the Euphrates. Walk through original ancient streets, houses, storerooms and temples seeing an unparalleled window into Late Chalcolithic city life.

3,400 years old · Late Bronze Age

Tell Munbaqa

Ancient Ekalte, a fortified Bronze Age town on the opposite shore. Its monumental architecture is remarkably preserved: stroll through real streets and enter houses that have stood for over three millennia.

Getting There

From Aleppo

A comfortable 1.5-hour drive from Aleppo International Airport brings you to the village of Al Kesra, where you park and board directly for the river trip.

The Journey

Sail surrounded by wild water birds, small fishing boats and a landscape of stunning beauty. The river and both sites are completely safe to visit.

What to Bring

Wear a hat and sport shoes. Bring your own food and water as there are no facilities at the sites.
